PRIVACY

Your information stays under your control.

Your documents and answers are used to build your plans. ActionBridge does not automatically send them to dentists, insurers, or employers.

You review before calculation Nothing is estimated until you’ve checked the facts it depends on.
You decide what gets saved Keep a plan to return to later, or close it and start fresh.
You control what gets shared Sharing a plan is always your choice, never a default.
